# Checklist: numbers you can defend

Run before a data memo leaves your hands. Written after a competitor gap analysis in which every figure had to cite its API call; these are the places the rule bit.

## Provenance

- [ ] Every figure in the text points at a saved call: `[call N]`, with `N` resolving to a file holding that call's input and output.
- [ ] Every keyword list you hydrated is diffed against what came back. Missing keywords are listed as "no measured volume." They are never silently absent.
- [ ] Sums show their terms. "620/mo cluster" is followed, somewhere, by the fourteen keywords that make it.
- [ ] Grouped spelling variants (model, modeling, modelling) are counted once.

## Positions

- [ ] Every competitor position is labeled by source: dataset or live, with the date.
- [ ] Dataset positions use the organic group rank. The absolute rank counts AI Overviews, People Also Ask and video blocks as positions; do not quote it as a rank.
- [ ] Any position quoted to leadership was checked live that day, or the memo says "dataset" next to it.

## Intent and homonyms

- [ ] Head terms checked for the other meaning: the aluminum company, the hybrid work model, the generic business example, the acronym that means three things.
- [ ] Navigational and transactional intent on a term lowers winnability for a blog post, and the score says so.

## Coverage

- [ ] Market support checked before a market is promised. The dataset serves some country and language pairs and not others; the error message tells you which.
- [ ] Zero rows is a finding, not a failure. Write down which competitors have no footprint and which phrasings have no demand.
- [ ] Sample caps are stated: which tools stop at 100 rows, and what the total was.

## Grounding

- [ ] Every "why us" line comes from the product knowledge base or a public product page. No entry means the line is cut or marked unverified, and an idea that depends on it drops.
- [ ] Anything the knowledge base marks as outside the product's scope is removed, whatever its volume.

## Record

- [ ] Cost recorded.
- [ ] Research log updated with what was bought and a do-not-re-buy date.
