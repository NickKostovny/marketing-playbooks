# Gates for any engagement list that goes to sales

Run these in order. Each one is free until the last.

- [ ] **Resolve and date first.** Know what the posts are before scraping. Seventeen variants of one campaign is one audience.
- [ ] **Capture count versus shown count.** Record both per post. One short is normal (hidden members). More than a few short means the modal stalled.
- [ ] **One row per person across all posts.** Multi-post engagers and commenters outrank single likes. A reaction other than a plain like is the intent tell.
- [ ] **Employer is a hypothesis.** Label parsed employers `inferred`. Carry a confidence column on the account roll-up. Never fill an employer you cannot see in the headline.
- [ ] **Drop non-prospects before the deal gate.** Own staff, competitors, suppliers, vendors, academics, consultancies.
- [ ] **Deal gate on every account. Blocking.** Active deal or partnership goes to a separate hold file named `HOLD-do-not-run`. Closed-lost is a revival, not cold. Unchecked accounts stay marked `UNCHECKED`, never treated as clear.
- [ ] **Persona gate on the headline, labeled as such.** Department match plus seniority floor plus exclusions. Expect near zero passes. Say the pass rate in the first paragraph of the deliverable.
- [ ] **Enrichment scope is the action tier only.** Target-department match or director and above, with a resolved domain. Everything else gets a reason in the `email_status` column, never a blank.
- [ ] **Verified and inferred never share a column.** Emails and titles from the enrichment tool are verified; everything from headlines is inferred.
- [ ] **Sanity-check the emails.** Two different people at one company returning the same address is a pattern guess. An alias that breaks the first.last pattern is evidence of a real record.
- [ ] **Read me tab first.** Every priority code and column explained in plain words. Tab names for the reader, not the pipeline.
- [ ] **State the audience finding.** Who the ads actually reached is worth more than the list.
