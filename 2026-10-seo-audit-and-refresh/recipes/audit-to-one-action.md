# Audit to one action

1. Read the project memory first (what the business does, goals, past research). Ground the report in it.
2. Check the research log. Reuse anything under 30 days old.
3. Crawl about 100 pages. Skip performance runs unless asked.
4. In parallel, pull backlinks and a traffic estimate.
5. Fetch the home page, robots file, sitemap, and every page you plan to name. Compare with what the crawl said.
6. Sort findings: broken or blocking first, then thin pages, then metadata length. Cap the list at ten. A beginner report with twenty items has failed.
7. Pick the one thing:
   - clean site, no links: outreach
   - best page has a stale year: refresh and keep the address
   - blocked or noindex pages: remove the block
8. Write it plainly: one action, why it matters, three steps, how to check it worked.
9. Gloss every term on first use. No drama words.
10. Write back to the project: corrected overview, key pages, one log line with what you bought.
