# One-pager rebuild brief

Use this when you rebuild an existing handout on a new design system. Paste it with the old PDF and the design system's README.

```
Rebuild the attached one-pager on the attached design system.

Copy: keep it word for word. You may only (1) join or split sentences where
the layout forces it, (2) rewrite a stat strip as sentences if the system has
no stat strips, and (3) write image captions from what the image visibly shows.
List every copy change you make.

Patterns: for every pattern in the old page that the system's guidelines
remove, name the pattern, say why it goes, and say what replaces it. Output
that as a table before you build.

Structure: give each side one job. Front: what it is, and one piece of real
evidence. Back: the objections a buyer raises, and how to start.

Print: [OFFICE PRINTER | PRINT SHOP: size, stock, bleed]. Body text 12pt or
larger, captions 9pt or larger, lines 1px solid, and images 300 dpi at placed
size.

Verify by measuring, not by preview: report page count and each sheet's
rendered height, and render each page to PNG.
```
