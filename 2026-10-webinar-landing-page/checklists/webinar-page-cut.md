# Cutting a webinar landing page to the decision

The reader decides in a few seconds. The page answers five questions and shows one button: what is it, when, how long, what does it cost, who is speaking. Start from the two sections below and treat anything else as a candidate for the white space, not a section.

## Keep

- Header: wordmark left, partner marks or one label right.
- Hero: a short label naming the organisations, the title as the publication wrote it, one sentence of lead (the publication's own description is usually best), a three-cell facts strip (date, time, length), and a card with the button and the "can't make it live" line.
- The panel: photo, name, company, title, bio as the listing has it. Company above title so cards level across title lengths.

## Cut, and why

| Section | Why it goes |
| --- | --- |
| A text label like "A [publication] webinar" | Folded into the header as the partner marks. |
| A separate recording note | Belongs in the register card, where it removes the "I can't attend" objection at the moment of decision. |
| A short speaker list in the hero | Duplicates the panel section. One speakers section. |
| "What you'll learn" bullets | Compress into the hero lead. |
| A point-of-view band | Promotional on a page the publication hosts, and it sits between the reader and the speakers. |
| A "speakers to be announced" card | An empty card weakens the panel. Name the company in the hero label and add the card when the speaker is confirmed. |
| A closing band | The page is short enough that the hero button is always close. |
| A footer | Out of scope for a page nobody navigates from. |

## Fill the white space with product

If the page needs more, add one thing that supplements the panel's thesis and shows the product, between the panel and the end: an existing demo that fits the topic, with one plain-English heading, three short paragraphs, and a Book a demo button with campaign UTMs. Caption simulated data as simulated.

## Before sharing

- The register link is the real one. Never invent a URL.
- Nothing implies the other speakers' companies endorse yours.
- Faces show on landing at a 900px-tall window. Measure the photo top; do not eyeball it.
- The copy has no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no superlatives, no capitalised eyebrows.
- 1px lines, no shadows, no gradients, no pills. Hover changes colour only.
- A visible 2px focus outline on every link and button.
- Noindex, not in the sitemap, not linked from the nav.
