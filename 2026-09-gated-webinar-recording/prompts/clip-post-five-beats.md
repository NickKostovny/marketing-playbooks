# Prompt: a LinkedIn post for a video clip, in five beats

The shape every clip post in this sequence used. About 70 words. The link never goes in the body.

```
Write a LinkedIn post to accompany this video clip. The clip transcript and the speaker's role are below.

Shape, in order:
1. The pain, hedged. One line. Something the reader may have felt, stated as "if you have ever..." or "most teams...". Not a claim about them.
2. The turn. One line that says what the speaker argues instead.
3. "Here, <role> ..." One line that captions the clip: who is speaking and what they cover.
4. One specific detail from the clip that makes it concrete. A phrase the speaker used, in quotes, under twelve words.
5. The ask. "Full session in the first comment." Nothing else.

About 70 words total. Plain sentences. No em dashes. No hashtags. No emoji. Name the speaker by role unless they are the public face of the session.

Then write the first comment: one line saying what the full session is and how long, followed by the URL with its source tag.
```

Why the link goes in the comment: LinkedIn suppresses posts with outbound links in the body. A clip post exists to be seen; the link exists to be clicked by the people who watched. Two jobs, two places.
