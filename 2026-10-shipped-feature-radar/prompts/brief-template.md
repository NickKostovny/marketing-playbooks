# Brief template for the drafting tool

Most failures in the first round came from the brief, not the tool. A brief with only the engineer's facts produces a post that assumes the reader knows the product. This template adds the three missing fields.

```
Top-of-funnel LinkedIn post. Reader: <persona> who has never used <company>.
What <company> is (use word for word as the first sentence): <approved one-liner>.
The AI: introduce it only by what it does, in one sentence ("<AI name> can now <does X>."). Do not write "<AI name> is our AI".
What is new, in the reader's words: <plain description from the engineer's post>.
Why it matters: <the pain, from the engineer's post>.
Limits to keep: <caveats from the post and its thread>.
Facts: use only the facts above. Do not invent consequences, scenes, or numbers.
Words to avoid: import, ingest, mapping, schema, deviation (for drift), session, prompt, AI assistant, copilot, agent, data platform, single source of truth, silo, button names.
No quote marks. No em dashes.
```

Before the first run: read the drafting tool's brand do/don't rules. A rule such as "lead posts with a quote" will produce invented quotes from any brief.
