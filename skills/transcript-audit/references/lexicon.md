# Lexicon: commonly mangled terms

A checklist of the terms that auto-transcription garbles on bioprocessing / RNA
talks, especially with accented speakers. The "heard as" column lists real or
likely phonetic substitutions; extend it whenever a new one is confirmed.

This is a starting checklist, **not a limit** — anything matching the
phonetic-substitution pattern should be caught even if it's not here.

## Core RNA / bioprocess terms likely to be garbled

Watch for phonetic variants of these; confirm the intended one from context.

- **mRNA / messenger RNA**, **IVT** (in vitro transcription), **LNP** (lipid
  nanoparticle — often heard as "LMP," "L and B," "element P")
- Nucleotides: **NTP, ATP, CTP, GTP, UTP** (single letters often split, e.g.
  "C T P"); **cap analog**, **capping reagent**, **5′ cap**
- Phosphates: **pyrophosphate, orthophosphate, monophosphate, triphosphate**
- **poly(A) tail**, **oligo-dT**, **template DNA**, **T7 RNA polymerase**
- Quality/process acronyms: **CQA** (critical quality attribute), **CPP**
  (critical process parameter), **CMA** (critical material attribute), **KPI**,
  **GMP** ("GMP-grade," "GMP-compliant"), **QbD** (quality by design)
- **dsRNA**, **capping efficiency**, **integrity / intactness**,
  **immunogenicity**, **cytokine**, **antigen**, **spike protein**, **EGFP**
- Buffers/ions: **HEPES, Tris**, **magnesium (Mg²⁺)**, **pKa**, **proton /
  hydrogen ion**
- Mechanism words: **nucleophilic attack**, **pentacoordinate**,
  **two-metal-ion / two-magnesium mechanism**, **heteropolymer**, **residence
  time distribution**

## Instruments, methods, units

- **HPLC**, **mass spectrometry**, **UV**, **fluorometry**, **conductivity**
- **unscented Kalman filter**, **Henderson-Hasselbalch equation**
- **CFD** (computational fluid dynamics), **techno-economic analysis**,
  **multiphysics modeling**, **soft sensor**
- Units are frequent error sites — check numbers that contradict each other, and
  watch °C, milliseconds, fold-changes (e.g. "2.3-fold"), R² values.
